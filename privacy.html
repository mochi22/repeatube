1. Overview

RepeaTube ("we", "our", or "the Service") is a free, browser-based tool that allows you to loop and control YouTube videos. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

RepeaTube does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. The Service runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate any servers or databases that store user data.

3. YouTube and Third-Party Services

RepeaTube uses the YouTube IFrame Player API to embed and play videos. By using this Service, you are also subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. YouTube may collect data about your video viewing activity in accordance with their own policies.

4. Cookies

RepeaTube itself does not use cookies. However, the embedded YouTube player may set cookies in accordance with Google's cookie policy.

5. Analytics

We do not use any analytics or tracking services.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
